How to play

Bubbles

Press the key shown on a bubble to pop it. A wrong key costs points and breaks your streak. If a bubble touches the surface line it counts as a miss, and the game ends when you run out of lives. Every 10 pops starts a faster wave.

KeyMagnet

Press the key that glows on the keyboard before its timer runs out. Faster correct answers earn more points. Pick a key set and a difficulty before you start; harder levels give you less time per key and more trials.

Controls

Start begins a game and turns into Pause during play. Restart begins a fresh session. Reset clears the board without starting. You can also tap bubbles and on-screen keys on a phone.
